Who am i?

My name is Azar and I was born the child of ordinary human parents, whom I loved dearly until the Third Demon War took them from me. In a moment of great despair, my magic powers ignited, and after the war I decided to become a powerful magician and protect the weak.

Eighty years and countless challenges later, I had become a powerful magician and protector, wandering the land to help wherever needed and learning continuously.

On my journey, I discovered secret techniques and powerful spells that, among other things, make it possible to stop aging or to change my face and appearance in the eyes of others. That way, I can also pass as an adventurer from time to time.
